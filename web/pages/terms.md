## The short version

AttendWise is an attendance tracker. It does not encourage anyone to skip classes. When it says a class is safe to skip, it is only doing arithmetic on the numbers you entered. Whether you attend is your decision, and so are the consequences.
:::

## 1. About AttendWise

AttendWise is an Android app, built by Priyanshu Debnath, that helps students record their attendance and see where they stand against an attendance requirement. By downloading or using the app or this website you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use them.

## 2. AttendWise does not encourage skipping classes

:::note
Attend your classes. Nothing in AttendWise is advice, a recommendation or encouragement to miss any class.
:::

Features such as safe skips, Can I bunk today, the day-off planner and the verdicts they show only calculate what your recorded attendance allows against the requirement you set. They exist so that you can avoid falling short by accident, not to tell you to stay away. The humorous tone of some notifications and messages is a joke and should not be read as encouragement to skip.

## 3. Your responsibility

Your institute, department and course instructors set the attendance rules that apply to you. These can differ between courses and can change during a semester. You alone are responsible for knowing and following them, and for checking your attendance against the official records your institute keeps. AttendWise is not an official record, and you should not present its figures as proof of attendance.

## 4. Accuracy

The numbers in the app depend on what you enter: your timetable, courses, the institute calendar, day swaps and the attendance you mark. Mistakes, missed updates or a rule the app does not model can make its results wrong. Treat every figure as an estimate and leave yourself a safety margin.
