## The short version

AttendWise keeps only what it needs to work: the courses, timetable and attendance you enter, and your sign-in. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold. You can ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted at any time.
:::

## 1. Who runs AttendWise

AttendWise is an independent project run by Priyanshu Debnath. For anything about your data, email [priyanshuib01@gmail.com](mailto:priyanshuib01@gmail.com).

## 2. What AttendWise stores

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| Sign-in | Your email address if you sign up with email. If you use Google sign-in, your Google account identifier and name. Handled by the sign-in service described below. |
| Profile | The name you enter, whether you follow first-year-only day swaps, and the notification voice you choose. |
| Your semester | Semester name and dates, including mid-semester dates. |
| Courses and timetable | Course codes, names, rooms, colours, the attendance percentage required, whether a course is never to be skipped, and the weekly class times. Extra classes you add. |
| Attendance | Whether you marked each class present, absent, cancelled or on duty, and any skips you planned. |
| Notification settings | Which reminders are on, reminder timing, quiet hours and the daily digest time. |

AttendWise does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not use an advertising identifier.

## 3. Where your data is stored

- **On your phone.** Everything you enter is stored in a database on your device. The app works from it, even with no internet, and syncs again when you are back online.
- **In the cloud.** A copy is synced to a database run on Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, India (region ap-south-1). Each person can read and change only their own rows. The institute calendar is shared and contains no personal data.
- **Operator access.** As the person who runs the service, I have administrative access to the database. I use it only to run, fix and secure the service.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in private.

## 4. Who else is involved

| Who | Why | What they see |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Runs the sign-in and the database for synced accounts. | Your sign-in details and the data listed above. |
| Google | Only if you choose Google sign-in. | That you signed in, as Google normally records. |
| GitHub | Hosts the website, the app downloads and the release list. The app asks GitHub for the latest release to offer updates. | Your IP address and the usual details of a web request. The app sends no account data to GitHub. |

There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. Your data is not sold or shared for marketing.

## 5. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show class reminders, mark prompts and alerts. |
| Exact alarms | To fire a reminder at the right minute. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, not by a server. |
| Run at startup | To set reminders again after your phone restarts. |
| Internet and network state | To sync your account and check for updates. |
| Vibration | For notification vibration. |
| Install apps | To install an update that you chose to download inside the app. |

## 6. This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics. Its fonts are hosted here. The Download button asks GitHub for the latest release, so GitHub sees your IP address when it does.

## 7. How long data is kept

Your synced data is kept until you delete your account. Signing out does not delete it. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. To delete your account and everything in it, follow the steps on the [delete account page](/AttendWise/delete-account.html). I delete it within 30 days of your request.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can view and change your information inside the app. You can ask me for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your personal data. To use any of them, or to raise a concern, email me and I will respond.

## 9. Age

AttendWise is made for college students. If you are under 18, please use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. For a significant change I will say so on the [changelog](/AttendWise/changelog.html).
