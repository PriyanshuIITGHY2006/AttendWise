You can have your AttendWise account and everything stored in it deleted at any time. This page explains how.

:::note
Signing out does not delete your data. To delete it, follow the steps below.
:::

## Delete your cloud account

1. Send an email to [priyanshuib01@gmail.com](mailto:priyanshuib01@gmail.com) from the email address you signed up with. If you use Google sign-in, send it from the Google account you signed in with.
2. Use the subject "Delete my AttendWise account".
3. I will check the request is from you, delete your account and confirm by email. This is done within 30 days of your request.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in and account.
- Your profile, semesters, courses, timetable and extra classes.
- Your attendance marks and planned skips.
- Your notification settings.

Deleting the account removes all of these together.

## Remove the data on your phone

Data stored on your phone is separate from your cloud account. Remove it by uninstalling AttendWise, or in Android Settings open Apps, then AttendWise, then Storage, then Clear data.
