## Reminders arrive late or not at all

- Open Android Settings, then Apps, then AttendWise, then Battery, and set it to **Unrestricted** or **Not optimised**. Phone makers often stop background apps to save battery.
- Check that **Notifications** are on for AttendWise, on the Android side and under **Profile** in the app.
- Check **Mute everything** and **Quiet hours** in Profile, and Do Not Disturb on your phone.
- On Android 13 and newer, allow the Notifications permission when asked.
- Restart the phone once. AttendWise sets reminders again after a restart.

## A widget is out of date

Open the app once, which refreshes all widgets. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again.

## The update will not install

- Allow AttendWise to install updates when Android asks, then tap **Update** again.
- Make sure you have enough free space and an internet connection.
- If it still fails, download the file from the [home page](/AttendWise/) and install it by hand. See [updating the app](/AttendWise/help/updating-the-app.html).
- If Android says the app was not installed, you may have the test build and be trying to install the real one, or the other way round. They are separate apps and one does not replace the other.

## The numbers look wrong

- Mark any past classes you have not marked. Unmarked classes are treated as classes you can still attend, so numbers are more hopeful until you mark them.
- Check the course's required percentage, and its **Runs for** setting. Half-semester courses need the mid-semester dates in your semester.
- Check that the timetable is right. Changes apply from today, so edit it if a class moved.
- Check **First-year UG** in Profile, because first-year-only day swaps change which classes run.
- Percentages are rounded down.
- Your institute's own record is the one that counts. See [safe skips explained](/AttendWise/help/safe-skips-explained.html).

## Sync does not work

Check your connection, then open **Profile** and tap **Sync now**. If it keeps failing, the message shown there helps when you report it. Changes stay on your phone and sync later.

## I cannot sign in

Check the email and password, or use **Forgot password?**. If you used Google to sign up, use **Continue with Google** again.
