## Add a widget

1. Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen.
2. Choose **Widgets**, then find **AttendWise**.
3. Drag the widget you want onto the screen. All of them can be resized.

## The five widgets

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Today | Today's classes with Present and Absent buttons. |
| Next class | Your next class, its room and a countdown. |
| Course ring | One course's attendance and safe skips. You choose the course when you add it. |
| Can I bunk today? | A one-line verdict for today. |
| Needs attention | Courses that are Tight or At risk. |

## Marking from a widget

The buttons on the Today widget write the mark straight away, without opening the app. The widgets refresh when you mark something, at midnight, when a class starts or ends, and after the timetable, settings or a sync change.

## The verdict is not advice

The "Can I bunk today?" widget is a one-line summary of what your numbers allow. It is not a recommendation to skip. See [safe skips explained](/AttendWise/help/safe-skips-explained.html).

## A widget looks out of date

Open the app once, which refreshes every widget. If it still looks wrong, remove it and add it again. See [troubleshooting](/AttendWise/help/troubleshooting.html).
