The Plan tab shows what taking certain days off would do to your attendance. It never changes anything until you apply a plan.

## Use it

1. Open the **Plan** tab and tap the days you want off.
2. Read the list. Every course that has classes on those days gets a verdict.
3. Untick any course you want to keep attending. By default every course with a Safe or Risky verdict is ticked, with all its classes on those days.
4. Tap the button at the bottom, for example **Plan 5 skips**, to save the plan. If the plan goes over what a course can take, the button reads **Plan 5 skips anyway**, so you cannot do it by accident.

## The verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Safe | All the classes on those days fit within the skips you have left. |
| Risky | Allowed, but you are below the requirement now, so skipping makes recovery harder. |
| Partial | There are more classes than skips left, so you must attend some of them. |
| Must attend | You have no safe skips left in this course. |
| Locked | The course is set to Never skip. |
| Nothing to skip | The course has no classes on those days. |

The banner at the top sums it up, for example that all the classes are skippable, that only some are, or that none are.

## Your plans

Applying a plan marks those future classes as planned skips. **My plans** lists them. Choose **Remove** to undo a plan and bring those classes back to normal. The evening before a planned skip, AttendWise can remind you, so you can change your mind.

Remember that a plan is an estimate based on your current marks and the institute calendar. Read [safe skips explained](/AttendWise/help/safe-skips-explained.html) for how the numbers are made.
