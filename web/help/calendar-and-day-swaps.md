## The Calendar tab

The Calendar tab shows a month. Tap a day to see its classes below the grid, and to mark or change them.

The small marks under a date tell you how the day went:

- All classes present.
- At least one class missed.
- A planned skip.
- A holiday.
- A day swap, shown with a swap arrow.

## The institute calendar

AttendWise keeps an institute calendar with holidays, exams, breaks and day swaps. It is shared by all users, and it contains no personal data. The app uses it to decide which classes happen on each day.

## What a day swap is

On a swap day the institute follows another weekday's timetable. For example, Thursday may run Friday's timetable. AttendWise then shows Friday's classes on that Thursday, and the Today screen carries a notice such as "Day swap: Friday's timetable today". You do not need to do anything.

## First-year swaps

Some swaps apply only to first-year students. Turn **First-year UG** on or off in **Profile** to follow them or not.

## Holidays and breaks

On holidays, mid-semester break and end-semester break there are no classes, so nothing is counted against you.

## Something looks wrong

The institute calendar is maintained by hand and the institute can change it at short notice.
