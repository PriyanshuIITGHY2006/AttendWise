### Does AttendWise tell me to skip classes?

No. It is an attendance tracker. When it says a class is safe to skip, it is only doing arithmetic on the numbers you entered, so you do not fall short by accident. Whether you attend is your decision. Please attend your classes. Read the [terms](/AttendWise/terms.html).

### Are the numbers official?

No. They are estimates based on what you enter and on the institute calendar. Your institute's own record is the one that counts, so check it and leave a safety margin.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. The app runs from the data on your phone, and syncs your changes when you are back online.

### Is it free?

Yes. AttendWise is free to download and use.

### Which institute does it support?

It is made for IIT Guwahati students, and its holidays and day swaps follow the IIT Guwahati calendar. You can use it elsewhere, but the institute calendar will not match your institute.

### Is there an iPhone version?

No. AttendWise is Android only, and needs Android 8.0 or newer.

### Is my data private?

Each account can see only its own data, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app. Read the full [privacy policy](/AttendWise/privacy.html).

### How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the [delete account page](/AttendWise/delete-account.html).

### Why is my percentage lower than I expected?

Classes you have not marked yet are treated as classes you can still attend, and percentages are rounded down. Mark your past classes and check the course settings. See [troubleshooting](/AttendWise/help/troubleshooting.html).

### Why is a holiday or day swap missing?

The institute calendar is maintained by hand. Please report the date so it can be fixed.

### How do I update the app?

It updates itself. When a new version is available, a bar appears above the tabs. See [updating the app](/AttendWise/help/updating-the-app.html).

### Who makes AttendWise?
