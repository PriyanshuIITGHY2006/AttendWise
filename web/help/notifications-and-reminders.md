All reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not need the internet and no server sends them.

## What AttendWise can send

| Notification | What it does |
|---|---|
| Class reminders | A reminder before each class you are expected at. The lead time is 10 minutes by default and you can change it. You get none for planned skips or cancelled classes. |
| Mark prompts | A prompt right after a class you have not marked yet, with Present, Absent and Cancelled buttons. |
| Daily digest | A summary of classes you have not marked yet, on days that have classes. It arrives at 9 pm by default. |
| Planned-skip reminders | A reminder the evening before a class you planned to skip. |
| Attendance alerts | A one-time alert when a course gets worse, from Safe to Tight or from anything to At risk. |

Turn each one on or off in **Profile** under **Notifications**. **Mute everything** switches them all off at once.

## Quiet hours

Set **Quiet hours**, for example 11 pm to 7 am. Class reminders that would fall in that window are dropped, because a late reminder is no use. Mark prompts and planned-skip reminders are not lost: they are delayed until quiet hours end.

## Voice

Choose how notifications sound in **Profile** under **Voice**.

- **Roast.** Deadpan and a little teasing, never mean.
- **Plain.** Facts only.
- **Kuchupuchu.** A clingy, playful Hinglish voice.

Every line still carries the real fact. The humour is a joke, not encouragement to skip.

## Permissions

On Android 13 and newer, allow **Notifications** when asked. AttendWise also uses exact alarms, so reminders arrive on the minute.

## Reminders arrive late or not at all

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. Open Android Settings, then Apps, then AttendWise, then Battery, and choose **Unrestricted** or **Not optimised**. Also check that notifications for AttendWise are on, and that Do Not Disturb is not hiding them. More in [troubleshooting](/AttendWise/help/troubleshooting.html).
