:::note
AttendWise shows what your recorded attendance allows. It is not advice to miss class, and its figures are estimates, not official records. Always check your institute's own attendance record.
:::

## The idea

Each course has a required percentage, for example 75. AttendWise counts every class in the semester, the ones already held and the ones still to come, and works out how many classes you could still miss and end the semester at or above the requirement.

## A worked example

A course has 40 classes in the semester and needs 75 percent.

- You must attend at least 30 of the 40 (75 percent of 40).
- So you can miss at most 10 classes in total.
- If you have already missed 4, you have **6 safe skips** left.

AttendWise uses whole-number arithmetic for these decisions, so rounding can never flip a result.

## What you see for each course

- **Current percentage.** Attended classes divided by classes held and marked so far.
- **Safe skips left.** How many more classes you can miss and still finish at the requirement, after the skips you have already planned.
- **Planned skips.** Future classes you have marked absent in advance. They use up your safe skips.
- **If you attend all.** The percentage you would finish on if you attend every remaining class.
- **Recovery.** If you are below the requirement, how many classes in a row you need to attend to get back to it. If even attending everything cannot reach it, the app says so.

## The three labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Safe | At or above the requirement with skips to spare. |
| Tight | At or above the requirement, but with no skips left, or every spare skip already planned. |
| At risk | Below the requirement now, unable to reach it, or planning more skips than the course can take. |

## Never skip

A course set to **Never skip** always shows zero safe skips, and the planner locks it. Use this for labs and instructors who do not allow absences.

## Glossary

- **Requirement or threshold.** The percentage you need in a course.
- **Safe skip.** A class you could miss without going under the requirement.
- **Planned skip.** A future class you have marked absent in advance.
- **Half-semester course.** A course that runs only before or after the mid-semester break.
