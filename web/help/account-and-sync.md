## Signing in

You need an account to use AttendWise. Sign in with Google, or sign up with your name, email and a password of at least 6 characters. Use **Forgot password?** if you lose your password.

## Sync

Your courses, timetable and marks are kept on your phone and copied to your account, so they survive a lost phone and appear on a new one.

- AttendWise syncs shortly after you make a change, when you open the app, and about every 6 hours while you are online.
- To sync right now, open **Profile** and tap **Sync now**.
- Profile shows when the last sync worked.

## Working offline

The app runs from the data on your phone, so everything works with no internet, and the changes sync when you are back online. If you sign in for the first time with no connection, the app may say it could not reach the server. You can **Try again**, or choose **Continue offline** to start with an empty account that syncs later.

## Signing out

Signing out removes your data from this phone. Changes that have not synced yet are lost, and everything that has synced stays in your account. Sync first if you are unsure.

## Delete your account

You can ask for your account and all its data to be deleted. The steps are on the [delete account page](/AttendWise/delete-account.html). Signing out does not delete anything.
